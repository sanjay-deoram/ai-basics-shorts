# SFX

Every sound in this folder is synthesized by `tools/make-sfx.mjs` (seeded noise and sine partials; no third-party
audio): `sub`, `key`, `keys`, `click`, `tick`, `stream`, `riser`, `scan`, `confirm`, `vague`, `pad`.
`lengths.json` holds their lengths in seconds and is read by `tools/build-sfx.mjs`.
