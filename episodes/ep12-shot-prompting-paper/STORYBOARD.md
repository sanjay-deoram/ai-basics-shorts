voice take, word timings and lip-sync as the IDE version (`../ep12-shot-prompting`); only the picture changes.

hard cuts to solid colour panels and dark "action" interludes).

## Look (`assets/paper.css`)

type, Inter for UI, Geist Mono for small labels. Words rise in with a soft blur (`assets/fx.js`).
Sanjay: a round cam disc at the bottom centre (x 440–640, y 1272–1472) for the whole short; the halo breathes with
the voice; smiles on the recap. Claude is the Claude logo (Thinking…, assistant turn, Labeling…).
