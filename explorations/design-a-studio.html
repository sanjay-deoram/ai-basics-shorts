Character

Meet Spark

A soft sphere of light drawn in the same gradient as the product visuals. Black ink eyes keep it in the notebook. It squishes, blinks, tilts, and glows instead of talking.

Spark

Spark is the AI on the other side of your prompt: very bright, very eager, and new to your job. Give it a vague ask and it squints. Give it a clear one and it glows.

Pose 1

Neutral

Listening. Slow breath, a blink every five seconds. Default between beats.

Pose 2

Confused

Shows up after a vague prompt. Tilts and sways, one brow up, a question mark bobbing.

Pose 3

Delighted

Shows up after the better prompt. Squash-and-stretch hops, a pink halo, ink sparkles.

Color

97% achromatic

Paper and ink carry every frame. Violet, ember, and blush appear only inside Spark and the product visuals it reacts to. Verdict green and red are separate and only mark Clear or Vague.
