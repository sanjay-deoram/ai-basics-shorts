Learn AI one line at a time.

Built from the look of sanjaydeoram.com: near-black canvas, off-white text, monospace everywhere, and an ASCII dragon as the host. One amber phosphor accent marks whatever the viewer should look at right now. Green and red only ever mean good prompt and bad prompt.

$ cat tokens/color.css

Color

Warm charcoal instead of pure black, bone instead of pure white. One accent. Pass and fail are reserved for verdicts and never used as decoration.

Neutrals
Ink#0d0c0bVideo canvas and page background.
Carbon#161513Terminal windows, prompt and output cards.
Graphite#24211eRaised chips, KPI tiles, empty progress.
Seam#34302bHairline borders and dividers.
Ash#948d83Labels, captions, secondary copy.
Bone#f2ede4Headlines, body text, Byte's outline.
Accent and verdicts
Phosphor#ffb547The only accent: Byte's eyes, the caret, tip cards, the word to look at.
Pass#7dd892"Clear" verdicts and the After label only.
Fail#ff6a5c"Vague" verdicts and the Before label only.
