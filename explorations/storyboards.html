Three ways to tell the same 60 seconds

Each storyboard teaches the same five habits from Anthropic's prompting guide, with a bad prompt and a better one for every habit. They differ in how the story is told. Thumbnails are plain wireframes, so any of the three designs can be dropped on top.

1Be specific 2Explain why 3Show examples 4Label the parts (XML tags) 5Say what to do, not what to avoid

How to ask for changes

Every frame has an ID like V2-F03. Reply with the ID and what to change, for example: V1-F06 · make the tags fly in one at a time or V3 · swap tip 4 for "give it a role". On the published page you can also leave a comment right on the frame.
