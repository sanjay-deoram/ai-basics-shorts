Style 1 · 45 seconds · 6 scenes

Whiteboard drawing

One black line drawing per scene draws itself stroke by stroke, then a short label fades in under it. The topic is "What is an LLM?" Every drawing finishes before its scene fades out.

Style 2 · 20 seconds · 4 steps

Isometric workflow

Each step is a paper block that rises into place. A Spark-colored dot travels along a path to the next block, and the block glows when the dot lands. The workflow shown is how an AI writes its answer.

0:00.0 / 0:20

Kept from your prompt

  • 20 seconds, one block per step
  • Blocks rise in, a dot travels to the next one
  • Plain-word labels, 54px on the 1080 canvas
  • No shadows, no neon, no particles, no camera spin

Eggshell touches

  • Eggshell, taupe and stone faces with ink hairlines
  • Step numbers printed flat on each block's top
  • Color only in the dot and the glow when it lands
