Three guides to replace Spark

Option A

Blink

The text cursor, come to life.

Every chat with an AI starts with a blinking cursor. Blink is that cursor with a face, so the series' guide is the thing viewers already stare at when they talk to Claude.

  • EnterGrows up from a line, like a cursor appearing
  • ThinkTypes its own thoughts as it moves
  • PointLies down and underlines the key word
  • IdleBlinks on and off, exactly like a cursor

Option B

Nodi

A face made of data points.

Embeddings, maps of meaning and neural networks are all dots joined by lines. Nodi is built from them, so it can come apart and re-form as whatever the lesson needs.

  • EnterScattered points fly together into a face
  • ThinkRearranges itself into a question mark
  • PointDraws a line out to the word and links it
  • IdleEvery point drifts gently, always alive

Option C

Bub

A chat bubble that talks back.

The speech bubble is the universal sign of a chat. Bub arrives like a new message, typing dots first, and the dots become its face.

  • EnterPops in as a typing indicator, then wakes up
  • ThinkTilts, looks up, a question mark pops out
  • PointStretches its tail out like a finger
  • TalkSquashes and stretches with every word

Fits the series

All three keep Spark's violet, blush and ember on the eggshell page, so every episode's colour rules stay the same: colour lives only in the guide and what it touches.

Talks with the voice

Each mouth reads the same lip-sync data Spark uses, opening wider on louder syllables. The mouths here run on a stand-in rhythm.
